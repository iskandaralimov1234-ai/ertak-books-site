Lola and the Blue Tea Bowl

A Gentle Story About Telling the Truth for Kids Ages 3–6

Ages: 3–6

While twirling in the garden, Lola accidentally breaks Grandma's favorite blue tea bowl. She hides the pieces and tells nobody, but her feet feel heavy, her snack tastes like nothing, and even the sparrow and the tiny ant seem to notice. When Lola finally finds the courage to tell the truth, Grandma shows her that honesty matters more than any bowl, and that broken things can be mended. A warm picture book with rhythm, repeated phrases and sound words, made for reading aloud with children ages 3–6.
