Questions parents ask

Are these children's books really free?

Yes. Every book on this site is free to read online and to download as a PDF or EPUB. There is no sign-up and no payment.

What age are the books for?

Our picture books are written for children ages 3-6 and work well as bedtime stories and read-aloud stories.

Which languages are available?

English, Russian and Uzbek. Open the language menu at the top of any page to read the same story in another language.

How do I read an EPUB?

Open the file in the Books app on iPhone and iPad, in Google Play Books or any e-reader app on Android, or on a Kindle through Send to Kindle. If you prefer, download the PDF instead.
